# Oregon Opportunity Zone 2.0 Map

Interactive map of the 58 census tracts on Business Oregon's "Oregon Opportunity Zone 2.0 – Final Designations" list (published October 28, 2026), shown on 2020 U.S. Census tract boundaries.

- Filter by rural / non-rural, county, or search by GEOID, city or nominating organization
- Click a tract for its county, nominating organization, land area and step-up type

It is a single static `index.html` (D3 loaded from cdnjs), so it deploys to Vercel with no build step.

Sources: Business Oregon final designation list; U.S. Census Bureau 2020 tract boundaries; county outlines from `us-atlas`. "Rural" reflects Oregon's classification on the list. For research only, not a tax determination.
